Altitude from pressure

At boot the board locks a pad reference p0. After that, altitude above the pad comes from the barometer alone. The altimeter on the left of this page uses the same formula.

h = 44330 × (1 − (p / p0)1/5.255)

Design decisions

Two I2C buses instead of one
Each sensor gets its own hardware bus, so neither can stall or collide with the other as sample rates go up.
IMU power and chip-select from GPIO pins
Driving chip-select high locks the IMU into I2C mode, and fewer separate wires means fewer connections to shake loose under vibration.
UART radio instead of an SPI one
This keeps the SPI bus free for SD card logging.
Pad zero before launch
After a settle delay the board averages 50 pressure samples, trims the highest and lowest 10 percent, and locks the result as ground reference. A dead sensor at boot halts the board in an SOS blink. Try "Reboot without the IMU".
